Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Corner Tales ("we", "the app") plays short audio stories about places near you. We built it to keep as much as possible on your iPhone. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Location and routes: used to find stories near you and start them when you arrive. Your walks, routes and location history are stored only on your device.
  • Listening history, saved stories, badges, quests and XP: stored only on your device.
  • Steps (Motion & Fitness): read from your iPhone's motion sensor to fill your daily rings. Never uploaded.
  • Profile name and photo: stored on your device. Your first name is shown on voice stories you choose to share.
  • Story writing: stories and quizzes are written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

Information sent to other services

  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation): to find places near you, the app sends your approximate coordinates to Wikipedia's public API. No account or personal identifier is included. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Apple Maps: place search, walking directions and Look Around use Apple's MapKit under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose Continue with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account identifier, handled by our authentication provider Supabase. We use these only to sign you in and attribute your shared voice stories.
  • Shared voice stories (optional): when you post a voice story while signed in, we store the recording, its transcript, title, your first name, the language, and the location where you recorded it, so people who walk past can hear it. Recordings are checked on your iPhone for offensive language before upload.

Speech recognition and microphone

The microphone is used only while you record a voice story. The recording is transcribed using Apple's speech recognition (on device where supported) to check it for offensive language and to show captions.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Reporting and moderation

Anyone can report a shared voice story. A story reported by three different people is hidden automatically and may be reviewed and removed.

Your choices

  • Use the app as a guest; nothing personal leaves your device except approximate coordinates sent to Wikipedia.
  • Turn off location, microphone, motion or notifications at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your voice stories, or use You › Account › Delete account and all data to erase everything on your device and remove your shared voice stories from our servers.

Children

Corner Tales is suitable for families. Children under 13 should use it with a parent, and should not post voice stories or sign in without a parent's permission.

Data storage and security

Shared voice stories and sign-in data are stored with Supabase using industry-standard encryption in transit. Access is protected by row-level security so only you can add or delete your own stories.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
